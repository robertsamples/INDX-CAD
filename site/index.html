Geometry reverse engineered from Prusa's printable model, which was licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (the original 3MF is included in the repository for archival purposes). The original model and its remixes were removed from Printables. Prusa's mesh was clean enough for parametric geometry to be extracted and converted to STEP.

The mechanism is complete — fillets, internal geometry, and the moving assemblies are all modeled. The repository contains the Fusion 360 archive (.f3z), a STEP export, and the original .3mf.
